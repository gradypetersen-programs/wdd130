New York

New York is a diverse state with a long history. From the longhouses of the pre-United States Native Americans to the goliath skyscrapers of New York City, the history of New York is adorned with innovation and progress. New York is home to a large population of white-tailed deer and other wildlife. Upstate New York is a welcome contrast to the busy city life, providing milk from hundreds of dairies and syrup from even more maple trees. Another unique characteristic of Upstate New York is it's proximity to Canada. With the Canadian border so close, travel to Canada and travel from Canada to New York is constant. Business, leisure, and hockey are aspects of shared culture that bring the two peoples together.
